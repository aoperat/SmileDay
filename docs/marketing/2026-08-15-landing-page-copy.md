## English

### Meta

- title: `SmileDay — Five-second smile breaks for your day`
- description: `An iOS habit app with reminders at your times and a five-second guide. No scores, no streaks, and every record stays on your phone.`

### Hero

- eyebrow: `A smile habit app for iOS`
- H1: `Smile, just because`
- sub: `A few times a day, five seconds at a time. For people who rarely smile — and would like to, a little more often.`
- CTA: `Get it on the App Store`
- CTA(secondary): `Read the story behind it`

### Empathy

- H2: `When your face says something you don't mean`
- body:

> You can mean well and still be misread. Your expression is the first sentence you hand people every day.
>
> A stiff back at least aches. A stiff face leaves no trace. If a stretch reminder can straighten one, a smile reminder can loosen the other.

### How it works

- H2: `It does one thing`
- steps:
  1. `A reminder arrives at your times` — you choose when, how often, and even the words.
  2. `A five-second guide keeps it easy` — it tells you what to do, so there's no freezing up.
  3. `The count goes up by one` — with the last 7 days and a monthly view to see the rhythm.
- caption: `Busy? Long-press the notification and tap "I smiled." It's recorded without opening the app.`

### Principles (4 cards)

- H2: `What we left out, on purpose`

| Card | Title | Body |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | No camera | The basic flow never uses the camera. The moment your face is on screen, practice turns into judgment. |
| 2 | No scores | No scores, no rankings, no streaks. A day without a smile is just an empty day, not a failure. |
| 3 | No nagging | Miss a reminder and nothing piles up. The next one simply arrives on schedule. |
| 4 | Nothing leaves | No server, no account. Every record stays on this phone. |

### Records

- H2: `Seeing the rhythm keeps it going`
- body: `Today's count, the last 7 days, a monthly view. Not a scoreboard — a way to see this small habit rolling along.`

### Live smile check (optional)

- H2: `Curious? There's a live check`
- body: `An optional mode that reads the movement at the corners of your mouth and shows it as a number, for that session only. It's a sensor reading, not a verdict — and nothing is saved: no photos, no video, no numbers. Available on Face ID devices.`

### Final CTA

- H2: `Start with five seconds`
- body: `You can still be someone who rarely smiles. Just also someone who's practicing.`
- CTA: `Get it on the App Store`
