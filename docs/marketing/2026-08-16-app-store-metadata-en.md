## Promotional Text

Room to smile, even on a busy day. Reminders at your times and a five-second guide make it easy to start.

## Description

How many times a day do you smile on purpose?

SmileDay is a simple habit app that helps you pause for a moment and smile, even in the middle of a busy day.

Get a reminder at the times you choose, then take five easy seconds to smile. Watch the small moments add up — today, the last 7 days, and a monthly history.

Main features

• Reminders at the hours and interval you set
• A five-second smile guide that's easy to follow
• Add and edit your own reminder wording
• Today's smile count at a glance
• The rhythm of your last 7 days
• A monthly history, day by day
• Record a smile right from the notification, without opening the app
• An optional live mode that shows your mouth-corner movement in real time

No scores to chase, no goals hanging over you. SmileDay keeps things short and easy to repeat, so the smiling moments in your day grow one at a time.

Start today, with a five-second smile.
