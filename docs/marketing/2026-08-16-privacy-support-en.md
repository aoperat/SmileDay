## /smileday/privacy — 아래에 추가할 영어 섹션

### Privacy Policy (English)

**SmileDay stores everything on your device. Nothing leaves it.**

**What SmileDay keeps**
- The time of each completed smile, and which guide ran.
- Your reminder schedule and any reminder wording you write.
- These are stored only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage.

**What SmileDay never does**
- No account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising identifiers.
- Nothing is sent to us or to any third party. The app makes no network requests with your data.
- Deleting the app deletes every record with it.

**About the optional live smile check**
- The front camera is used only while you run it, to read how far your mouth corners have moved. The iOS green camera indicator is visible the whole time.
- No photos or video are captured or saved. The camera image is never read as a picture.
- The live signal, timeline, and ratio you see exist only in memory and disappear when you close the screen. They are never stored or transmitted.
- The score is a sensor reading, not a judgement of your face or emotion.

**Notifications**
- Reminders are scheduled locally on your device through iOS. We have no server and cannot see them.

**Contact**
- Questions about this policy: see the support section below.

## /smileday/support — 아래에 추가할 영어 섹션

### Support (English)

**Getting help**
- Email us and we'll reply as soon as we can. We answer in English or Korean.

**Common questions**

*Reminders stopped arriving.*
Open iOS Settings → Notifications → SmileDay and check that notifications are allowed. The app's home screen will also point this out and offer a shortcut.

*Can I change the reminder wording?*
Yes — Settings → Manage messages. Add, edit, delete, or reorder. Your own wording stays exactly as you wrote it, in whatever language you wrote it.

*Does the live smile check save my face?*
No. It reads mouth-corner movement only, keeps no photos or video, and its results disappear when you close the screen.

*Where is my data?*
Only on your iPhone. There is no server. Deleting the app deletes all records.
