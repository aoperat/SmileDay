### 프로모션 텍스트 (170자 이내)

```

```

### 설명

```
How many times a day do you smile on purpose?

SmileDay is a simple habit app for the days that go by without one. It sends a reminder at the times you choose, walks you through five easy seconds of smiling, and keeps count.

WHAT IT DOES
• Reminders at the times and intervals you choose
• A five-second guide, so you're never left wondering how
• Write and edit your own reminder messages
• Today's count at a glance
• The last 7 days, and a monthly view
• Record straight from the notification — no need to open the app
• An optional live mode that shows how far the corners of your mouth have moved

NO PRESSURE, BY DESIGN
No scores, no rankings, no streaks. A day without a smile is just an empty day, not a failure. Reminders you miss are never resent or stacked up.

THE CAMERA IS NOT PART OF THE HABIT
The basic flow — reminder, guide, count — never uses the camera. The optional live mode reads only the movement at the corners of your mouth, shows it as a number for that session, and saves nothing: no photos, no video, no measurements.

YOUR DATA STAYS YOURS
Everything is stored on this phone. There is no server and no account, and nothing is ever uploaded.

Start with five seconds today.
```

검수 메모:

- 금지어 검사 완료 (lift/tone/firm/anti-aging/rejuvenate/wrinkle/therapy/treatment/cure/heal/depression/anxiety/mood disorder/clinically 없음).
- "no streaks"는 부정형 언급이라 의도적으로 허용. 스트릭을 압박 장치로 쓰는 게 금지인 것이지, 없다고 말하는 건 차별점이다.
- 실시간 모드 문단은 TrueDepth 심사 대응 논리(선택형·프레임 비독취·비저장)를 그대로 옮겼다 (`docs/reports/2026-08-07-app-review-truedepth-response.md` 참조).

## 5. 보도자료 보일러플레이트

**한국어**

> 스마일데이(SmileDay)는 잘 웃지 않는 사람을 위한 iOS 습관 앱이다. 정해둔 시간의 알림과 5초 가이드로 하루에 몇 번 의식적으로 웃어보는 시간을 만들고, 그 횟수를 기록한다. 점수·순위·연속 기록을 일부러 두지 않았고, 기본 사용 흐름에 카메라를 쓰지 않으며, 모든 기록은 서버 없이 기기에만 저장된다. 1인 개발 브랜드 돌파로(dolparo)가 만들었다. https://dolparo.com/smileday

**영어**

> SmileDay is an iOS habit app for people who rarely smile during the day. With reminders at chosen times and a five-second guide, it makes room for a few deliberate smiles a day and keeps count. It deliberately has no scores, no rankings, and no streaks; the basic flow never uses the camera, and every record stays on the device — there is no server. SmileDay is built by dolparo, an independent one-person studio. https://dolparo.com/smileday
